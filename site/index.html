01 / About

Hey, I'm James.

The dad behind the handle.

I'm working on my health while doing the same things a lot of us are: raising a family, working, and figuring out what to make for dinner.

I share my weight-loss journey because I want it to be useful to someone else. The food, the workouts, the things I'm learning—it all has to fit into an actual life.

This is a place for my recipes and honest notes along the way. I'm sharing my experience, not prescribing a plan for yours.
