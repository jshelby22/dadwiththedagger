BBQ jalapeño chicken

Chicken in a creamy BBQ sauce with onion, jalapeño and a little smoked paprika.

How to make it

  1. Pat the chicken dry; do not rinse it. Toss with smoked paprika, garlic powder and black pepper. Keep raw-chicken utensils separate from ready-to-eat ingredients.
  2. Heat 1 tsp oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Brown half the chicken for 4–6 minutes, turning occasionally; transfer to a plate for partially cooked chicken. Repeat with remaining oil and chicken. It will finish cooking in the sauce.
  3. Reduce to medium heat. Add onion and jalapeños and cook 3–4 minutes. Add a splash of the measured broth if they stick.
  4. Add the remaining broth and BBQ sauce, scraping up browned bits. Lower the heat and whisk in cream cheese a few cubes at a time until smooth, about 2–3 minutes. Do not boil hard.
  5. Return chicken and any juices to the pan. Cook gently 3–5 minutes, checking the thickest pieces for 165°F (74°C). Turn off heat and stir in shredded cheese until melted. Taste before adding salt; thin with water, 1 tbsp at a time, if needed.
  6. Divide the chicken and all the sauce evenly into four servings.

Before you cook

This is a developed recipe idea, not a dish James has tested. One seeded jalapeño makes a milder version; leaving some ribs adds heat. Rice, broccoli or a baked potato are optional sides, not part of the four servings. Refrigerate leftovers 3–4 days and reheat to 165°F; the dairy sauce may turn grainy after freezing.

Storage & reheating

Keep cooked meat or poultry leftovers in a refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below and use within 3–4 days. Freeze any portions you won't use in that window. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C), checked with a food thermometer.
