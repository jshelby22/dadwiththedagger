Cajun cream chicken

Chicken, bell pepper and onion in a creamy Cajun-inspired skillet sauce.

How to make it

  1. Pat chicken dry. Toss with 1 tsp Cajun seasoning and black pepper; reserve the second teaspoon for the sauce.
  2. Heat 1 tsp oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Brown half the chicken for 4–6 minutes, turning; move to a plate for partially cooked chicken. Repeat with remaining oil and chicken.
  3. Reduce to medium heat. Cook onion and bell pepper 4–5 minutes, adding a splash of the measured broth if they stick. Add garlic and remaining Cajun seasoning and cook 30 seconds.
  4. Add the remaining broth, scraping the pan. Lower heat. Whisk in softened cream cheese gradually until smooth, about 2–3 minutes. Stir in hot sauce; avoid a hard boil.
  5. Return chicken and juices to the pan. Cook gently 3–5 minutes, checking several large pieces for 165°F (74°C). Remove from heat and stir in cheddar until melted. Taste before adding salt.
  6. Divide chicken, vegetables and all sauce into four servings. Add water by the tablespoon if the sauce is too thick.

Before you cook

This is a developed recipe idea, not a dish James has tested. This is Cajun-inspired, not a traditional preparation. For less heat, use 1 tsp Cajun seasoning total and omit hot sauce. Rice or green beans are optional. Refrigerate leftovers 3–4 days and reheat to 165°F.

Storage & reheating

Keep cooked meat or poultry leftovers in a refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below and use within 3–4 days. Freeze any portions you won't use in that window. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C), checked with a food thermometer.
