Crispy chicken with creamy dill pickle sauce

Oven-baked panko chicken strips with a separate cheesy dill-pickle dipping sauce.

How to make it

  1. Preheat a conventional oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a sheet pan with parchment rated for that temperature. Cut chicken into even ½-inch-thick strips and pat dry.
  2. Mix panko with olive oil, smoked paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder, black pepper and optional salt. Put beaten egg in a second shallow bowl. Keep raw-chicken utensils separate from finished food.
  3. Dip chicken into egg, let excess drip, then press into panko. Space strips in a single layer. Bake 18–22 minutes, turning halfway, until browned and the thickest strips reach 165°F (74°C). Check early; thickness matters. Discard leftover egg or coating that touched raw chicken.
  4. While chicken bakes, warm milk and cream cheese in a small saucepan over low heat, whisking 3–5 minutes until smooth. Take off heat and stir in cheddar and the remaining ½ tsp garlic powder.
  5. Stir in drained pickles, fresh dill, optional hot sauce and 1 tbsp pickle brine. Taste before adding the remaining brine. Thin with water by the tablespoon if needed.
  6. Divide chicken and all sauce into four servings, keeping the sauce alongside for dipping rather than soaking the coating.

Before you cook

This is a developed recipe idea, not a dish James has tested. Keep cooked chicken and sauce separately in the refrigerator for 3–4 days. Reheat chicken in a 375°F oven and warm sauce gently, checking both for 165°F. Freezing may change sauce texture. Cabbage salad or roasted potatoes are optional sides.

Nutrition estimates in the source are planning estimates from representative ingredient values, not verified labels. They count all measured egg and panko even if some coating is discarded. No macro totals are published here. Contains milk, egg and wheat; check packaged ingredients for additional allergens.

Storage & reheating

Keep cooked meat or poultry leftovers in a refrigerator at 40°F (4°C) or below and use within 3–4 days. Freeze any portions you won't use in that window. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C), checked with a food thermometer.
